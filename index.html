What to expect at a jam

Escojam is a night for hearing live music, meeting other music lovers, and sharing the stage. Come to sing, rap, or play, or spend the evening listening.

If you’re thinking about performing for the first time, get in touch and we’ll talk you through how to take part.

Meet the “ESCO JAM”

Meet the founders and musicians behind the collective.

Joseph

Drums

I play many instruments, but drumming is my passion. I started playing on pots and pans at the age of three and kept it going through choirs as I grew up. My biggest inspiration is none other than the King of Pop, Michael Jackson. My ambition is to publish my music, build relationships with artists and producers, and grow a network of creatives. I want Escojam to be a global powerhouse.

Josh

Bass

I fell in love with bass after watching Sharay Reid and haven’t stopped since. I love music because it connects us, and I look forward to growing this into gigs and festivals.

Tomi

Keys

I started playing drums in church from the age of 8 and picked up the keys shortly after. My musical inspiration is Stevie Wonder. My vision for Escojam is to meet like-minded music lovers and create a community.

A word from the crowd

Frequently asked questions

Can I come just to listen?

Yes. You’re welcome to enjoy the live music, meet people and spend the evening in the audience.

How do I sign up to perform?

Get in touch and tell us whether you’d like to sing, rap or play. We’ll talk you through how to take part.

Do I need to bring my own instrument?

Tell us what you play when you get in touch so we can confirm what equipment is available and what you’ll need to bring.

Where can I find event and ticket details?

Check The Next Jam for updates on the date, venue, doors and tickets.
