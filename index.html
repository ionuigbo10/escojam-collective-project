Esco jam Collective is a live music jam night and open mic event series in the London and Kent area focused on R&B, neo-soul, jazz, and hip hop. Our goal is to provide a social environment for people to network, enjoy good music and showcase their talents. So come play, sing, rap and express yourselves!
